

E-Commerce Platform – Business Requirements Document
## (BRD)
This Business Requirements Document (BRD) defines the functional and operational requirements for the proposed
E-Commerce Platform. The platform is designed to provide customers with a smooth online shopping experience while
enabling administrators to efficiently manage products, orders, inventory, payments, and customer interactions. The
system will support essential e-commerce capabilities including user registration, product browsing, shopping cart
management, secure payment processing, order tracking, reporting, and responsive mobile-friendly access.
